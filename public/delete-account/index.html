Delete your Momig account

Momig, by MVR Studios · Last updated 27 November 2026

You can delete your Momig account and its data at any time. You don't need the app installed: Momig also runs in any web browser.

Option 1: delete it yourself (immediate)

  1. Open momig.app in any browser, or open the Momig app.
  2. Sign in with the email address and password for the account you want to delete. Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen.
  3. Go to Settings and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you can't sign in, email privacy@momig.app with the subject "Delete my Momig account". Send it from the email address you use for Momig, so we know the request is really from you. We'll delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your name, email address, password and settings.
  • Everything you logged: food, water and movement, including entries you'd already removed.
  • Your Momig Plus trial or early-access status.
  • The copy of your data on that device.

Deleting your Momig account doesn't affect a Bebig account: they're separate.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups and provider logs: if our database host keeps backups, your data drops out of them within 30 days. Technical logs at our providers expire on their own schedule, typically within a few weeks.
  • Purchase records: once paid subscriptions start, records needed for accounting are kept as long as the law requires (in Finland, usually up to 6 years).

Subscriptions: deleting your account doesn't cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions first. (Paid subscriptions aren't available yet.)

Delete some data, but keep your account

  • A single entry: open it (tap it on Today or in All entries) and tap Remove.
  • Stop tracking something: switch the tracker off in Settings. What you logged is kept until you delete it or your account.
