1. Who we are

Momig (the app at momig.app and, later, the Momig app on Google Play) is made and run by Marija Riba, an independent developer based in Finland who trades as MVR Studios (Finnish Business ID 3485332-7). She is the controller of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). In this policy, "Momig", "we" and "us" mean her.

Questions about privacy: privacy@momig.app.

2. What we collect

You give us

  • Account details: your email address, your name if you add one, and your password (stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form).
  • What you log, only for the trackers you switch on: food (what you had, when, and calories if you add them), glasses of water (when, and the glass size), and movement (the kind, for example a walk, and how many minutes).
  • Settings: which trackers are on, a daily calorie goal if you set one yourself, whether gentle notes and Hide numbers are on, your water goal and glass size, and your time zone (so "today" is your day).
  • Messages to us: anything you send to our support or privacy addresses.

What you eat, drink and how you move can reveal information about your health. We treat all of it as health data, a special category of personal data under the GDPR. Log only what you're comfortable storing.

Created when you use Momig

  • Consent record: when you agreed, at sign-up, to the Terms and to Momig storing your entries.
  • Plan details: whether you have Momig Plus (through early access, a trial or, later, a subscription), with start and end dates. When paid subscriptions start, we'll also store the purchase reference Google Play gives us. We never see or store your card or payment details.
  • Security records: the technical logs our providers keep when you connect, such as IP address, device and browser type, and time.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and Momig doesn't set cookies. We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or anything else from your phone.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account. Momig can't work without storing your entries, so withdrawing consent means we stop processing and delete them. Withdrawing doesn't make earlier processing unlawful. Switching a tracker off stops new entries of that kind but keeps what you already logged; delete entries or your account to remove them.

We don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing. Momig never suggests calorie targets, and the totals and notes it shows are calculated only from what you log and the goals you set yourself.

4. Who can see your data

  • Only you. Every entry belongs to your account, and the database itself refuses anyone else, not just the app.
  • Our service providers, listed in the next section, only to run Momig for us.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

We never sell your data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it for marketing by others. Momig and Bebig are separate apps with separate accounts and databases.

6. Where your data is stored

Your account and entries are stored in the EU (Germany). Some providers above are US companies, and some data (such as connection details) may be processed in the United States. When that happens, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, with additional safeguards such as encryption in transit.

7. AI

Momig doesn't use AI today. Nothing you log is sent to an AI model. If we add an AI advisor later, it will be optional and off by default, we'll ask for your separate consent first, and this policy will say exactly what is sent and to whom before it's switched on.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account and entries: for as long as your account exists.
  • Deleted entries: a deleted entry disappears from the app straight away. A deleted copy stays in the database so your other devices can sync the deletion, until your account is deleted.
  • Provider technical logs: as set by each provider, typically a few days to a few weeks.
  • Backups: if our database host keeps backups, deleted data drops out of them as they're replaced, normally within 30 days.
  • Purchase records (once subscriptions start): as long as accounting law requires, which in Finland is usually up to 6 years.
  • Emails to us: as long as needed to help you, and no more than 2 years after the conversation ends.

9. Deleting your data

  • A single entry: open it and tap Remove.
  • Your account: Settings → Delete account, in the app or at momig.app in any browser. It takes effect immediately and deletes your account, settings, every entry and your plan details. Full steps, including how to ask by email, are at momig.app/delete-account.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data (you can edit your entries and settings yourself in the app);
  • delete your data;
  • take your data with you (email us and we'll send you a copy in a common format; an export button in the app is coming);
  • restrict or object to some processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time.

Email privacy@momig.app from your account's email address to use any of these rights. We'll reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Finland that's the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman. You can also contact the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Access to every entry is checked by the database itself, so no one else can see your data. Sign-up and password reset are protected by CAPTCHA, and the app only loads code from its own site and the CAPTCHA provider. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Who Momig is for

Momig is for adults aged 18 or over. It isn't meant for children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

13. Data on your device

To work offline and stay fast, Momig keeps a copy of your entries and settings in your browser's or phone's storage (IndexedDB), and your sign-in session in local storage. Signing out removes them. If you share a device, sign out when you're done.

14. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app or by email before they apply. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
